CORRECTIONS & AMENDMENTS

THE FIFTH BELL WAS NOT A TYPOGRAPHICAL ERROR.

In yesterday's edition we described the bell as striking five times at the fourth hour. This was correct. We regret any subsequent inquiries.

THE EDITORIAL OFFICE IS CLOSED WHEN THE BELLS RING.
