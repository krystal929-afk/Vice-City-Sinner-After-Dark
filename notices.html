HARBOUR OFFICE / NOTICE 014

TIDAL MARKER WITHDRAWN FROM SERVICE

The brass gauge opposite 18 Pelican Row is no longer maintained by the municipal surveyor. All new readings are to be taken at the main pier. The remaining coastal stations are unaffected.

THE REGISTER OF APPROVED STATIONS HAS BEEN CORRECTED.
TO LET / FURNISHED ACCOMMODATIONS

THE BELLWETHER ROOMS

No. 18 Pelican Row. Rooms furnished in the modern manner. Private bathing facilities, shaded verandas and fresh sea air. Inquiries received in person on Fridays after six.

RESPONSES MUST BE ADDRESSED TO NO. 18 PRECISELY.
MUNICIPAL DIRECTORY / A CORRECTION REQUEST

PELICAN ROW ENDS AT NO. 16

The latest civic survey lists eight houses on Pelican Row, numbered 2 through 16. Several readers have requested that No. 18 be restored to the register. No such entry appears in the original survey.

THE ADVERTISEMENT AT LEFT HAS NOT BEEN AMENDED.
✳FROM THE ORIGINAL MUNICIPAL SURVEY, 1944
EDITOR'S NOTE / FOR THE UNINITIATED

THE ARTICLES ARE NONFICTION. THE NOTICES ARE NOT.

Vice City Sinner publishes factual articles, essays and interviews. The Bellwether Rooms, Pelican Row notices and other invented entries belong to the fictional newspaper universe. Their repeating details are deliberate pieces of a story, not reports about real places or events.
