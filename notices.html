ARCHIVED HISTORY · MAY 20, 1927 · MIAMI BEACH
1927

MIAMI BEACH USED AN ELEPHANT AS A GOLF TEE.

Meet Rosie. A photograph preserved by the State Archives of Florida is cataloged as “Rosie being used as a golf tee.” The elephant was part of the extraordinary promotional spectacle surrounding early Miami Beach.

This is not a local legend. The state archives date the photograph to May 20, 1927.

PRIMARY RECORD: STATE ARCHIVES OF FLORIDA / VIEW THE 1927 PHOTOGRAPH ↗
RECENT REPORT · OCT 6, 2026 · MIAMI INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT
10

THE AIRPORT INCIDENT INVOLVING TEN BIRDS AND ONE PAIR OF UNDERWEAR.

According to a federal criminal complaint described by local news outlets, a traveler arriving from Cuba carried ten living songbirds inside plastic tubes sewn into his underwear. Customs officers discovered the concealed birds during an inspection.

The allegations are reported as allegations, not findings of guilt.

SOURCE: NBC 6 SOUTH FLORIDA / OCTOBER 6, 2026 ↗
RECENT REPORT · OCT 2, 2026 · KEY LARGO
333

A CARTOON CAT IN SUNGLASSES TURNED UP ON A COCAINE BRICK.

Monroe County sheriff’s deputies reported an approximately one-kilogram, duct-taped package of cocaine found in mangroves near John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park. Its marking was Tom the Cat wearing sunglasses.

Another package discovered in the Keys bore the number 333. Reporting says markings can be used to identify shipments.

SOURCE: MIAMI HERALD REPORTING / OCTOBER 2–3, 2026 ↗
